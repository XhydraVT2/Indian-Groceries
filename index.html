Since 1996

Started as one tin shop on a corner in Pune.

Apna Dukaan began as a single shop counter where our founder, Rameshbhai, weighed spices by hand and remembered every regular's order. Three decades and one app later, we still buy the way he did — direct from growers, in small batches, tasted before they're shelved.
